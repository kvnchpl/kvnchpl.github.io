About this index & sources

The index is a historical snapshot of reviews from 1999–2019, with a few newer albums I’ve written about.

Catalog data is adapted from P4KxSpotify by Anthony T. Pinter, Jacob M. Paul, Jessie Smith, and Jed R. Brubaker (2020), under CC BY 4.0, with duplicate entries removed and my reviews added. Review and artist links were recovered from the authors’ archive and The Fork. This is an independent project, unaffiliated with Pitchfork.
