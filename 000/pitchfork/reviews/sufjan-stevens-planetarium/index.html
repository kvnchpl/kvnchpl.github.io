Planetarium

Sufjan Stevens
released 2017 · pitchfork review 2017.06.13
Kevin9.1
Pitchfork6.0

Probably one of Sufjan's most underrated executions of the "concept album" that he is so well-known for. For all of Planetarium's intricate and compulsively dense allusions to Greco-Roman mythology, astronomy, Christian theology, astrological symbolism, queer longing, etc. etc., its solar system framework keeps the entire project containable and accessible. There is such an excellent balance of sprawling ambience, percussive intensity, and simply gorgeous instrumentation that it is hard to write this project off as mere avant-"affect". And when it comes to music, isn't affect enough, especially when it is this thoughtful and multifaceted?
