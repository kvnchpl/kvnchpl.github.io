The Ascension

Sufjan Stevens
released 2020 · pitchfork review 2020.09.25
Kevin8.2
Pitchfork7.0

Despite this being Sufjan's "pop album," or at the very least his rejoinder to pop music, it features some of his most affecting and idiosyncratic songs – within an exceedingly affecting and idiosyncratic discography. Even the album's more unassuming tracks carry this weight. From the menacing ostinato of "Die Happy" to the more jovial "Landslide," the songs are packed with engaging pop sensibility and yet have plenty of breathing room to luxuriate in. The Ascension is an album from a musician who is at the top of his game, still willing to explore the depths of sorrow and the heights of euphoria with the entirety of his sonic toolbelt.
