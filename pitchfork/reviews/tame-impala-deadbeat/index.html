a review by Kevin Cunanan Chappelle

Deadbeat

Tame Impala
Kevin7.3
Pitchfork4.8

A month out from the release, many of the songs that I found compulsively listenable no longer have the staying power that they once did. The sound design across the album is as meticulous and satisfying as expected, but the songcraft is admittedly rote this time around. However, there is something undeniably alchemic about Deadbeat, with Kevin Parker distilling many of Tame Impala's beloved qualities further into the realms of pop and techno. It is a transitional album that hints at a greater transformation that I am eager to witness.
