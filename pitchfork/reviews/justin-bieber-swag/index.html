SWAG

Justin Bieber
Kevin7.8
Pitchfork*7.3

Bieber at his most focused, and most effective. SWAG, while occasionally littered with trivial and mundane interludes, favors R&B cohesion over his usual, sprawling pop aimlessness. His collaborators as well as his inspirations loom heavily over the album, guiding forces that appropriately apply the prodigy's talents towards a much more earnest place. It is an album both sparkling and subterranean, and a rare and unexpected comeback for a pop star who has existed in the cultural imagination mainly as an untouchable phenom, and oftentimes an object of derision; finally he re-enters the stage with glimmers of himself.
