Age of Adz

Sufjan Stevens
Kevin9.4
Pitchfork*8.4

Following the success of Illinois, Sufjan makes a sharp turn into the volatile, paranoid world of The Age of Adz. Markedly more digital than its predecessor, it is no less whimsical and majestic despite its frequently glitchy aggression. The outstanding final track, "Impossible Soul," alone is a wandering, boundless fantasia, and could easily hold its own as a solid EP. Overall, The Age of Adz feels less plaintive and more pained – "I'm not fuckin' around!" he wails over and over. "Do yourself a favor and get real, get right with the Lord," he urges. The stakes here are urgent; the caustic blips and siren-like horns a fitting soundtrack to an increasingly apocalyptic world.
