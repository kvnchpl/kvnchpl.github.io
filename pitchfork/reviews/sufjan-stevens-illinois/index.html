a review by Kevin Cunanan Chappelle

Illinois

Sufjan Stevens
Kevin9.6
Pitchfork9.2

An utter masterpiece of well-deserved acclaim, and the triumphant culmination of Sufjan's classical era. Illinois is adorned with unimpeachable songwriting coupled with some of the decade's most exciting evolutions of indie folk music. It is a theatrical journey that takes you through multifaceted explorations of how the personal, spiritual, and historical inevitably intertwine. Listening to Illinois makes you feel as though you had experienced multiple lifetimes of joy, despair, confusion, and hope, all channeled through the specific lens of "Illinois." Grandiose yet vulnerable, there are so few other albums that can boast such an effortlessly holistic presentation of life.
