About this index & sources

Historical snapshot: P4KxSpotify review metadata dated 1999–2019, alongside six personal reviews. This is not the complete or current Pitchfork archive. Separate review dates remain separate records; edition labels are not inferred.

*Scores have not been independently verified against Pitchfork. The six personal reviews retain their supplied scores and original review links. Other scores come from P4KxSpotify v1.0, by Anthony T. Pinter, Jacob M. Paul, Jessie Smith, and Jed R. Brubaker (2020), used under CC BY 4.0. Changes: metadata extraction, duplicate removal, and association with personal reviews. The dataset has no original review URLs; imported records link to their dataset source. No Pitchfork writing or artwork is reproduced. Import date: November 2, 2026.

Import audit: 18403 source rows; 10 duplicate metadata rows collapsed; 1 row missing an artist omitted. Three explicitly matched records retain their personal-review identities.
