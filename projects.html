ABOUT

Kevin Cunanan Chappelle is an artist based in Brooklyn. Working across images, installations, interfaces, and writing, they build structures for experiences that resist being contained: memory, desire, grief, belief. Cameras, code, poems, and rituals become ways of translating between an inner world and a shared one. Their work follows the places where those translations become uncertain.
