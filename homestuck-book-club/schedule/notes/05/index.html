Reading overview

Jade’s Entry

  • Karkat begins trolling a John who already knows him. Rose and Kanaya work through their out-of-order conversations, while Dave uses time loops to help the other kids.
  • John retrieves his server disc and connects as Jade’s server player.
  • Bec’s prototyping saves Jade from the meteor, but also gives Jack First Guardian powers, making him a threat to both sessions.
  • After her dreamself’s death on Prospit, Jade meets Feferi in a dream bubble. Their dream replays an earlier conversation, but Feferi now tells Jade that she is dead.

Rose’s Plan

  • Rose plans to destroy the Green Sun to cut off Jack’s powers. John would retrieve the Tumor, a bomb inside the Battlefield, and Dave would map the route through the Furthest Ring. Rose’s dreamself would carry the bomb there; she does not expect to return.

Ascension

  • Vriska guides John to his Quest Bed. Jack kills him there, and his dreamself ascends to god tier.
  • Terezi shows Dave a possible route to god tier involving a doomed version of himself. Dave refuses to kill that other self.
  • A flashback reveals Vriska’s earlier ascension: Tavros leaves her dying on her quest cocoon, and she returns at god tier.

Creating Jadesprite

  • With Dave’s replacement equipment and shared grist, Jade makes new weapons and tools on the Land of Frost and Frogs.
  • Looking for a way to fight Jack, Jade combines her dead dreamself with Becsprite, creating Jadesprite.
  • Jadesprite’s panic sends the laboratory tumbling off the house. Jade tries to calm her as she cries among the wreckage.

Reading notes

  • Karkat expects an easy target and instead meets a John who already treats him as a friend. Here, Karkat is beginning a relationship that John has already experienced. His embarrassment makes the earlier insults feel different: the hostility is tangled up with affection.
  • Rose and Kanaya’s conversation is the eighth for both of them. Both can now remember the seven conversations that led up to it. After so much withholding and sarcastic maneuvering, I like the relief of seeing them finally reach that point.
  • The server disc stranded in Dad’s car becomes useful at last. The problem of getting hold of a game disc still matters in a story now concerned with making universes. Following a familiar object helps me connect the current events to the much smaller problems that started the story.
  • In her explanation of the Jack disaster, Vriska argues that she could not simply prevent an event that had already happened. That leaves another question: does knowing the outcome excuse the choices she made to bring it about?
  • John’s ascension looks triumphant, while Dave refuses to kill his doomed self when Terezi pushes him to confront what reaching god tier involves. Jade tries a different route to a powerful ally: bringing back her dreamself as a sprite. But Jadesprite’s distress makes the cost to that dreamself impossible to ignore. The new power comes with another person’s needs, and Jade hasn’t planned for them.

Open threads

  • Rose’s plan relies partly on an informant who wants to die. Dave questions whether she should trust him. Whether his advice serves the kids’ interests as well as his own remains unresolved.
  • Feferi tells Jade that she is dead. The loss of her dreamself has been explained, but how and when her waking self died have not been shown.
  • Jade hoped her revived dreamself could help fight Jack, but Jadesprite is crying in the wreckage of the laboratory. How the two Jades can work together remains unresolved.

Discussion questions

  1. Which old object or running joke had the most satisfying payoff in this section?
  2. How did you react to Karkat trying to troll a John who already thinks of him as a friend?
  3. Rose and Kanaya can both remember their previous seven conversations when they reach the eighth. What does finally sharing that history let them say or do differently?
  4. How is Rose’s plan supposed to stop Jack, and what roles do John and Dave have in it?
  5. What do you think will happen when the trolls’ countdown reaches zero? Which clues support your guess?
  6. Does the fact that an event has already happened excuse helping to cause it? How convincing is Vriska’s defense of her role in Jack’s rise?
  7. John reaches god tier; Dave refuses to kill his doomed self. What does each scene suggest about the difference between advancing in a game and making a choice you can live with?
  8. How do John’s death on the Quest Bed and return at god tier compare with ideas of reincarnation or rebirth? What changes when rebirth comes with greater power and a new role?
  9. Jade wants a powerful ally, but Jadesprite is distressed by being brought back. What responsibility does Jade have toward her dreamself when their needs conflict?

Trivia & Details
