Reading overview

Jade and Prospit

  • Jade Harley lives on a remote island with her dog Becquerel. She uses colored strings to remember her errands and the packages she expects to arrive.
  • When Jade falls asleep, her dreamself flies over Prospit while her dreambot moves around on Earth. She sees visions in Skaia’s clouds. John’s dreamself lives in the neighboring tower and has yet to wake up.
  • In the future, the Peregrine Mendicant and Wayward Vagabond help send a blue package into the past. John’s gift reaches Jade when she is still a little girl, years before he sends it.

Rose’s Entry and John’s First Gate

  • John searches Dad’s room and realizes he was wrong about his father being a professional clown. He makes new gear with alchemy while Rose builds his house up toward the first gate.
  • Rose finds a Skaianet lab that tracks Sburb sessions and incoming meteors. An attempt to retrieve a living Jaspers from the past triggers the appearifier’s paradox safeguard. It produces slime that another machine grows into a mutated clone. Rose later retrieves his original body from the mausoleum and uses it to prototype her sprite.
  • Bro leaves the Sburb discs behind after his rooftop fight with Dave. Dave connects as Rose’s server player, and she enters the Medium before the meteor strikes.
  • John jumps through his first gate. Jade enters the frog ruins on her island and finds Sburb discs in a lotus time capsule.

The Midnight Crew’s Raid

  • After the Felt hit one of their casinos, Spades Slick and the Midnight Crew raid the Felt’s mansion to find Lord English’s vault.
  • The Felt use time powers against the Crew. Trace can follow where someone has been and Fin can follow where they are going. The Crew avoids killing Snowman because her death would destroy the universe.

Inside the Vault

  • Slick pries open the vault and is thrown into another timeline, where the mansion is in ruins and almost everyone is dead.
  • Snowman tears off Slick’s barcoded arm and locks him inside. He flips his character graphic to move the barcode to his remaining arm, then scans it to open a hatch.
  • Below the hatch, Slick finds a command station with a troll on its screen and starts typing commands.

Reading notes

  • I love that Jade can see some things coming but still needs strings tied around her fingers to remember what to do about them. The strings make her foreknowledge feel like one more thing she has to keep track of.
  • The story asks us to “be” a character as if we’ve just picked them in a game. Then a command tells Jade to give up an interest, and she answers with a whole fantasy about becoming a wolf. I love how quickly the command gets drowned out by what Jade actually cares about.
  • One page pairs Jade in her dream room with her robot standing beside her sleeping body. Seeing both rooms together makes Prospit feel as much a part of her life as her room on Earth.
  • I love that the Intermission turns time into something you can track around a room. Slick realizes that a clock he saw broken in the future is still ticking in front of him. Now the reader and Slick are waiting for the same moment.
  • When Sawbuck is wounded, he and the people nearby jump to another time. That’s a great problem for Slick to solve. The jokes about Sawbuck’s size pull me out of a scene that already has a funny setup.

Open threads

These threads are unresolved at the end of this assignment.

  • The discs in the lotus. Jade finds two juice-stained Sburb discs inside a time capsule. How they got from Dave’s apartment to the ruins on her island is still unexplained.
  • The trolls’ access to the kids. GA confirms that she can contact Rose at different points in her timeline. The comic has not yet explained the trolls’ location or how they can reach across time.
  • Lord English. The Felt work for the unseen owner of the vault. The raid ends without revealing who he is or why his name carries so much weight.
  • The terminal under the vault. Slick’s command station shows a troll, much as WV’s showed John. The story hasn’t explained why the two stations monitor different groups.

Discussion questions

  1. Which detail of Jade’s life on the island most changed your first impression of her?
  2. What does seeing Jade on Prospit explain about the things she seems to know ahead of time on Earth?
  3. What does Slick figure out when he sees the same clock broken and then intact? How does he use that information?
  4. Slick looks like Jack Noir and finds a command station like the exiles’. What connection do you think the story is setting up?
  5. Jade ties strings around her fingers to remember what to do and when. What do you rely on to remind you at the right moment? What can a reminder system still leave you to figure out?
  6. When Jade refuses the command to give up her interest in animals, does it feel like she’s arguing with her own thoughts or with someone giving her instructions? What makes you read it that way?
  7. How do the jokes about Sawbuck’s size affect the time-travel comedy? What is the reader being invited to laugh at?
  8. How does the Intermission’s use of clocks and visible time trails compare with Act 3’s cuts between the kids? When does knowing the order of events create suspense, and when does uncertainty help?

Trivia & Details

  • Jade’s dog, Becquerel, shares his name with a unit of radioactive activity. One becquerel equals one decay per second.
  • The Midnight Crew’s four names use the suits of playing cards. The Felt’s numbered hats follow pool-ball numbering and colors.
