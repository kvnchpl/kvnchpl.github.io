Reading overview

Exploring the Medium

  • John explores the Land of Wind and Shade after passing through his first gate. Jade’s Grandpa appears and saves him from monsters.
  • On the Land of Light and Rain, Jaspersprite explains Rose’s quest. The planet’s fish have been eaten by the Denizen, and figuring out how to bring them back is part of the quest.
  • Jade helps Dave enter the Medium as his server player. Dave has to recover his entry egg from Crowsprite’s nest before his meteor hits.

A Doomed Timeline

  • GC offers John a shortcut. In one future, it leads to John’s death and leaves Jade without a server player in time for her entry.
  • Future Dave travels back to warn John and becomes Davesprite. John abandons the shortcut. Rose remembers parts of that future after her future self goes to sleep.
  • Rose destroys her first gate and ends her walkthrough. She sets out to find her own way to save the session.

The Paradox Clones

  • John reaches a laboratory in the Veil and uses its equipment to clone the four guardians as babies. The machine then combines their genetic material to produce four more babies: John, Jade, Rose, and Dave.
  • The babies travel to Earth’s past on the Reckoning meteors. They grow up to become the same kids and guardians whose genetic material and actions made their arrival possible.

Jack’s Coup and the Fall of Prospit

  • The Parcel Mistress bargains with Jack Noir to recover a package. Jack opens it, uses the weapon inside to kill the Black Queen, and takes her ring and its powers.
  • Jack kills the Black King and uses the White King’s scepter to start the Reckoning. Meteors from the Veil head toward Skaia, whose defense portals send many of them to different points in Earth’s history.
  • Jack attacks Prospit and sends its moon crashing into the Battlefield. Jade’s dreamself throws John clear and dies in the crash. John’s dreamself wakes on the Battlefield and finds her body and takes the White Queen’s ring.
  • The Parcel Mistress delivers the package to John. The armed bunny inside drives Jack away. The story also connects the people caught in the war to the exiles on Earth, including the Warweary Villein becoming the Wayward Vagabond.

Reading notes

  • The trolls do not know everything. CG has been working backward through John’s adventure, so he expects John to remember conversations they have yet to have from John’s side. I love how quickly all that knowledge stops looking like an advantage when he has to figure out whether John is joking.
  • GA and Rose turn conversation into a puzzle. It is Rose’s second conversation and GA’s seventh. GA sends Rose a transcript of a conversation Rose has yet to have, hoping the irritation it causes will turn into a friendship. I find it funny and oddly sweet that she explains the whole scheme to Rose as she’s carrying it out.
  • Alchemy makes snooping easy. Dave uses his alchemiter to copy Rose’s journals. I like how casually the comic turns a physical diary into something you can duplicate like a file. Rose can keep the originals and still lose control over who reads them.
  • Ectobiological weirdness. The person making the babies is one of the people being made. I keep thinking about how the loop changes the meaning of family roles we’ve taken for granted. John can call Nanna his grandmother and still owe his genetic origins to her in a completely different way.
  • The story can interrupt itself to explain itself. The first recap and second recap let us revisit old scenes with new information. Then there’s the author interlude, which turns the person explaining the story into someone who can interrupt it.

Open threads

  • The voices beyond Derse. Rose says the gods from the Furthest Ring asked her to burn the code. Why they wanted it destroyed and what else they want from Rose remain unclear.
  • Jade after Prospit. John finds Jade’s dreamself dead after Prospit falls. What that loss means for Jade when she wakes is still unknown.
  • WV’s ring. The act ends by revealing that WV has a royal ring. How it came into his hands is still missing from the story.

Discussion questions

  1. Which Act 4 reveal changed how you read an earlier scene?
  2. Why does John’s death in the doomed timeline put Jade’s entry at risk? What does future Dave change when he goes back?
  3. Jack has the Black Queen’s ring, and John has the White Queen’s. What do you think Jack will do next, and what might stop him?
  4. Rose destroys her first gate and starts looking for her own answers. What could she gain by leaving the route Sburb lays out for her? What might she lose?
  5. Do you think of Dave and Davesprite as the same person? What makes their different experiences matter to your answer?
  6. Dave copies Rose’s journals without taking the originals. Does copying a private journal feel different from taking it? How does that comparison carry over to private messages and files?
  7. How does the ectobiology reveal complicate words like “parent” and “grandparent”? When genetics and the work of raising someone point to different people, which family terms still fit?
  8. What changes when the person explaining the story shows up as a character in it? Does the author interlude change how you read the two recaps?

Trivia & Details

  • Jade stores objects by drawing them. She sketches her lunchtop on the Pictionary scribblepad to captchalogue it. The comic describes her switch to Pictionary as a democratic choice, a nod to the audience’s role in choosing commands.
  • AND and OR are different experiments. Dave combines the same ingredients with && and || and gets different results. It is a nice concrete example of a small change in notation creating a different object.
  • GristTorrent borrows from file sharing. Dave uses it to download shale from John. The comic even measures the transfer in grist per second.
