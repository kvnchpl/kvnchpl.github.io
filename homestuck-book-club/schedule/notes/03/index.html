Reading overview

Exploring the Medium

  • John explores the Land of Wind and Shade after passing through his first gate. Jade’s Grandpa appears and saves him from monsters.
  • On the Land of Light and Rain, Jaspersprite explains Rose’s quest. The planet’s fish have been eaten by the Denizen, and figuring out how to bring them back is part of the quest.
  • Jade helps Dave enter the Medium as his server player. Dave has to recover his entry egg from Crowsprite’s nest before his meteor hits.

A Doomed Timeline

  • GC offers John a shortcut. In one future, it leads to John’s death and leaves Jade without a server player in time for her entry.
  • Future Dave travels back to warn John and becomes Davesprite. John abandons the shortcut. Rose remembers parts of that future after her future self goes to sleep.
  • Rose destroys her first gate and ends her walkthrough. She sets out to find her own way to save the session.

The Paradox Clones

  • John reaches a laboratory in the Veil and uses its equipment to clone the four guardians as babies. The machine then combines their genetic material to produce four more babies: John, Jade, Rose, and Dave.
  • The babies are destined to travel to Earth’s past on the Reckoning meteors. These are the kids and guardians as infants, not additional copies who will grow up alongside them.

Jack’s Coup and the Fall of Prospit

  • The Parcel Mistress bargains with Jack Noir to recover a package. Jack opens it, uses the weapon inside to kill the Black Queen, and takes her ring and its powers.
  • Jack kills the Black King and uses the White King’s scepter to start the Reckoning. Meteors from the Veil head toward Skaia, whose defense portals send many of them to different points in Earth’s history.
  • Jack attacks Prospit and sends its moon crashing into the Battlefield. Jade’s dreamself throws John clear and dies in the crash. John’s dreamself wakes on the Battlefield, finds Jade’s body, and recovers the White Queen’s ring.
  • The Parcel Mistress delivers the package to John. The armed bunny inside drives Jack away. The story also connects the people caught in the war to the exiles on Earth, including the Warweary Villein becoming the Wayward Vagabond.

Reading notes

  • CG has been working backward through John’s adventure. Each conversation leaves him remembering exchanges John hasn’t had yet. I love how quickly access to John’s future stops looking like an advantage when CG has to work out whether John is joking.
  • Rose is having her second conversation with GA, while GA is having her seventh with Rose. GA sends Rose a transcript of a conversation Rose has yet to have, hoping the irritation it causes will turn into a friendship. I find it funny and oddly sweet that she explains the whole scheme to Rose as she’s carrying it out.
  • Dave uses his alchemiter to copy Rose’s journals. I like how casually the comic turns a physical diary into something you can duplicate like a file. Rose can keep the originals and still lose control over who reads them.
  • The person making the babies is one of the people being made. Nanna is John’s grandmother in the family that raised him, but the machine also makes her one of his genetic parents. I like how the reveal complicates that family relationship without making the family life established earlier any less real.
  • The two in-story recaps reorganize earlier scenes around what the reader has since learned. The author interlude makes the person explaining the story a character who can also interrupt it.

Open threads

  • Rose says the gods from the Furthest Ring asked her to burn the code. Their motives and reliability have not yet been established in the reading.
  • John finds Jade’s dreamself dead after Prospit falls. The assigned pages have not yet explored what that loss means for waking Jade.
  • The act ends by revealing that WV has a royal ring. How it came into his hands is still missing from the story.

Discussion questions

  1. Which Act 4 reveal changed how you read an earlier scene?
  2. How does John’s death in the doomed timeline prevent Jade from entering? What does future Dave change by going back?
  3. Jack has the Black Queen’s ring, and John has the White Queen’s. What do you think Jack will do next, and what might stop him?
  4. Rose destroys her first gate and starts looking for her own answers. What could she gain by leaving the route Sburb lays out for her? What might she lose?
  5. Do you think of Dave and Davesprite as the same person? What makes their different experiences matter to your answer?
  6. Dave copies Rose’s journals without taking the originals. Does copying a private journal feel different from taking it? How does that comparison carry over to private messages and files?
  7. How does the ectobiology reveal complicate words like “parent” and “grandparent”? When genetics and the work of raising someone point to different people, which family terms still fit?
  8. What changes when the person explaining the story shows up as a character in it? Does the author interlude change how you read the two recaps?

Trivia & Details

  • The narration describes Jade’s switch to Pictionary as a choice arising from the “ethers of democracy,” acknowledging audience input into the comic’s commands.
  • The alchemy operations AND (&&) and OR (||) use the same notation as the logical AND and OR operators in JavaScript.
  • GristTorrent echoes the name and file-sharing vocabulary of BitTorrent. The latter is a protocol in which people downloading the same files also upload data to one another.
