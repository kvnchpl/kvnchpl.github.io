Act 4: Flight of the Paradox Clones

Recap #3

Each house brought into the game sits on its own planet in the MEDIUM. These worlds are part of a small cosmos called the INCIPISPHERE, with SKAIA at its center and golden PROSPIT nearby. Beyond the players’ planets lie the VEIL, an asteroid belt, and purple DERSE.

Each player has seven gates to travel through. John and Jade’s dreamselves live on Prospit’s moon; Rose and Dave’s live on Derse’s.

Recap #3

John’s FIRST GATE drops him onto LOWAS, the LAND OF WIND AND SHADE. PM guides him through the local salamanders’ postal system so he can send DAD a replacement hat and request his missing Sburb disc.

Exploring nearly gets him killed. Jade’s GRANDPA appears, very much alive, shoots the monsters attacking John, and leaves without explaining his presence in the Medium.

Recap #3

Rose’s house has landed on LOLAR, the LAND OF LIGHT AND RAIN. MOM immediately wanders off, leaving Rose to fight monsters and track down JASPERSPRITE.

Jaspersprite tells her that figuring out what her quest means is part of the quest. Rose would rather know who wrote the rules in the first place.

Recap #3

Dave and Jade are still on Earth, and Dave needs her help to enter Sburb. Jade uses the lotus discs to connect as his SERVER PLAYER. Working through her dreambot while asleep, she prototypes his sprite with the impaled crow, creating CROWSPRITE.

Crowsprite steals Dave’s entry item, a red egg, and builds a nest on the radio tower. With a meteor on the way, Jade upgrades Dave’s alchemy setup while Dave tries to recover the egg.

Recap #3

Dave climbs the tower, but Crowsprite pecks him off. BRO’s rocket board catches him.

Bro rides the incoming meteor and slices it in half with his katana, buying Dave a few more seconds. The egg finally hatches, bringing Dave’s apartment into LOHAC, the LAND OF HEAT AND CLOCKWORK.

Recap #3

The trolls stop merely heckling and start meddling. gallowsCalibrator (GC) offers John a shortcut straight to his DENIZEN, the powerful creature waiting at the end of his planetary quest.

GC helps him turn an old alchemy experiment into a working rocket pack. John heads for the SEVENTH GATE, intending to skip most of his quest and kill the denizen in its sleep.

Recap #3

The shortcut leads to a dead end. In one alternate future, John is killed by his denizen. Without him, Jade never enters the Medium. Dave and Rose spend four more months gathering equipment and information, but cannot finish the game without their friends.

Dave decides to use his time powers to return to the day John died and warn him. Rose stays behind and falls asleep, hoping her dreamself can preserve something of this doomed timeline.

Recap #3

Future Dave returns just after his younger self enters the Medium. He gives his gear to his past self, jumps into Crowsprite, and becomes DAVESPRITE.

With two Daves now warning him, John abandons the shortcut and heads for his SECOND GATE instead. Davesprite carries the failed future’s experience into a timeline where his friends still have a chance.

Recap #3

Something from that discarded future survives. Rose inherits fragments of her future self’s memories, and her dreamself finally wakes on DERSE.

Her walls are covered in the MEOW message Jaspers whispered years ago. It is a genetic code she has been writing without knowing why. Dream Dave’s walls, by comparison, are covered in drawings of his webcomic, SWEET BRO AND HELLA JEFF.

Recap #3

John’s Second Gate takes him into Rose’s bedroom on LOLAR, but she is asleep. Their first in-person meeting fails to happen.

He finds her unfinished birthday gift, a patched version of the Con Air bunny, and takes it with him. He also impersonates Rose in a chat with grimAuxiliatrix (GA), leaving her a conversational mess to untangle, and trades his salamander for her mutant kitten before leaving.

Recap #3

Dave uses alchemy to copy Rose’s private journals, including her wizard story and the MEOW genetic code. He marks his place with the juice-stained Sburb envelopes.

Meanwhile, voices from the FURTHEST RING, the void beyond Derse, tell Rose to burn the code. These beings speak to Derse dreamers, as Skaia shows visions to Prospit dreamers. Rose destroys her copy. Dave’s duplicate leaves the dangerous information in circulation.

Recap #3

The DRACONIAN DIGNITARY, one of Jack Noir’s agents and Derse’s counterpart to Diamonds Droog, steals the journals from Dave’s room. A Dave who travels back to stop the theft is killed.

The surviving Dave comes home to his own corpse and decides against making the same trip. Time travel saved John, but it does not make every mistake safe to undo.

Recap #3

Rose gives up on following the game’s intended path. While Dave advances through his FIRST GATE, she deliberately destroys hers and sets out to find a way to salvage their session.

She ends her GameFAQs walkthrough and preserves it on a server in the FURTHEST RING, where players in other worlds might find it.

Recap #3

The guardians’ separate journeys converge at a laboratory in the VEIL. DAD, MOM, and GRANDPA leave aboard Grandpa’s battleship, bound for Skaia. They leave Mom’s pony, MAPLEHOOF, behind.

A Dersite official, the AUTHORITY REGULATOR, reaches the same lab while following DREAM CAL, the puppet Rose threw out of Dream Dave’s tower. He takes BRO’s rocket board and heads into the asteroid belt.

Recap #3

John reaches the Veil laboratory after the others have left. Like the lab Rose explored on Earth, it can extract PARADOX SLIME from people who cannot safely be taken out of the past, then use that material to create life through ECTOBIOLOGY.

The equipment is already locked onto NANNA, GRANDPA, BRO, and MOM. John collects their genetic samples and activates the machine.

Recap #3

The machine produces baby versions of all four guardians. Then it combines Nanna’s and Grandpa’s genetic material to make JOHN and JADE, and Bro’s and Mom’s to make ROSE and DAVE.

John has just created himself, his friends, and their family. These are their actual infant selves, destined to reach Earth by meteor and grow into the people we already know.

The family tree is now a circle.

Recap #3

carcinoGeneticist (CG) tells John that he did the same thing in the trolls’ session. But he also warns that JACK NOIR will start the RECKONING, the game’s meteor bombardment, before the kids are ready to face it.

John is busy reenacting the reunion from Con Air with the babies. Each latches onto an object or animal that will eventually reach Earth with them. Baby Jade gets Rose’s patched bunny.

Recap #3

While the kids explore, a delivery dispute is drawing the kingdoms into trouble. The PARCEL MISTRESS, a Prospitian mail carrier, is trying to deliver Jade’s birthday gift for John, a green package. It has been confiscated and sent to JACK NOIR, Derse’s resentful archagent.

Jack demands the WHITE KING’S and WHITE QUEEN’S crowns in exchange for the parcel. He gives the Mistress a sword to help her obtain them. Two acts of treason for one delivery.

Recap #3

Jack finds a more direct route to power inside the package: a weapon capable of killing the BLACK QUEEN. He turns it on her and takes her RING OF ORBS FOURFOLD.

Each prototyping completed before a player enters gives the ring’s wearer another set of features and powers: harlequin, cat, tentacles, and crow. The kids’ choices have armed a murderous usurper. The disgruntled bureaucrat has become the session’s biggest problem.

Recap #3

The WHITE QUEEN willingly gives the Parcel Mistress her crown and ring, then sends her to collect the WHITE KING’S crown. She chooses abdication over a fight.

The COURTYARD DROLL, another of Jack’s agents, steals the ring. Dream Jade clobbers him and takes it for safekeeping. Unlike Jack, she gains no powers from wearing it.

Recap #3

The conflict reaches the BATTLEFIELD at Skaia’s center, which the prototypings have expanded from a chessboard into a planet. Prospit and Derse wage their endless war across its surface.

When his farm burns, the WARWEARY VILLEIN rallies black- and white-shelled soldiers against both kingdoms. His rebels march on the BLACK KING. For a moment, this seems promising.

Recap #3

Jack interrupts the rebellion. He kills the BLACK KING, breaks the king’s scepter, and massacres the rebels. Only the Villein survives.

Jack’s red energy tears across the Battlefield. Replacing the king has made everything worse.

Recap #3

The WHITE KING has also surrendered his crown and scepter. The scepter falls into the hands of Jack’s agents, who bring it to him.

Jack uses it to start the RECKONING. Meteors launch from the Veil toward Skaia, forcing the kids into the game’s endgame before they are remotely ready.

Recap #3

Skaia defends itself by opening portals that send the meteors to different moments in time on Earth. The ectobiology lab places the eight babies and their belongings on separate meteors just before launch.

The babies are sent into Earth’s past so they can grow up and eventually help create themselves. The future Exiles also reach Earth through Skaia’s portals. The apocalypse that threatened the kids’ homes is also the delivery system that put everyone there in the first place.

Recap #3

The AUTHORITY REGULATOR finds another lab in the Veil, inside an intact version of Jade’s frog temple. The lotus time capsule is here too.

Its monitors show Nanna and Grandpa’s childhood in BETTY CROCKER’s household. Much of their care falls to the dog HALLEY, the next target of the lab’s equipment.

Recap #3

The DRACONIAN DIGNITARY arrives with the journals he stole from Dave. He drops the juice-stained Sburb envelopes into the lotus capsule, where they will wait millions of years for Jade.

Rose burned her copy of MEOW, but the Dignitary has Dave’s. He combines the code with Halley’s genetic material, creating BECQUEREL in a burst of green energy. Jade’s dog, discs, and ancient frog ruins all come from this meteor.

Recap #3

The Regulator flees Bec’s birth and returns to John’s laboratory meteor. John is asleep, the meteor is falling, and its transportalizers no longer work.

He straps John to BRO’s rocket board and launches him toward LOWAS. With no escape left for himself, the Regulator rides the meteor through one of Skaia’s portals to Earth.

Recap #3

Jack turns from the Battlefield to PROSPIT. He attacks the golden kingdom and cuts the chain holding its moon, sending it falling toward Skaia. Dream Jade and the still-sleeping Dream John are caught in the destruction.

On LOHAC, BRO confronts Jack directly and holds him off with a sword.

Recap #3

Dream Jade cannot wake John before the moon hits. At the last moment, she throws him clear and dies in the impact. Her DREAMBOT explodes on Earth, but waking Jade survives.

Dream John finally wakes on the ruined Battlefield. He finds Jade’s body and takes the WHITE QUEEN’S RING from her hand.

Recap #3

The PARCEL MISTRESS finishes her delivery mission amid the wreckage. After killing Jack’s henchman, the HEGEMONIC BRUTE, she trades both white crowns to Jack for the green package.

She carries it straight to Dream John, just as Jade’s instructions required.

Recap #3

Inside is Jade’s gift: the patched bunny, rebuilt as a heavily armed ROBOT BUNNY with help from her mysterious penpal. It is the same bunny John gave Baby Jade in the lab, now returned to him after years of upgrades.

Jack comes for the ring. The bunny uses its powerful weapons to drive him off and protect John.

Recap #3

GRANDPA lands on the Battlefield, leaves MOM and DAD there, and recovers Dream Jade’s body. He preserves her as a memorial, in accordance with the family’s unsettling taxidermy tradition, before eventually returning to Earth to raise her younger waking self.

Another souvenir in his laboratory is the FOURTH WALL, stolen from Jack’s office. Homestuck’s creator, ANDREW HUSSIE, appears through it to recap his own story.

Recap #3

The carapacians’ timelines finally line up. The WARWEARY VILLEIN becomes the WAYWARD VAGABOND. The PARCEL MISTRESS becomes the PEREGRINE MENDICANT. The AUTHORITY REGULATOR becomes the AIMLESS RENEGADE. The abdicated WHITE QUEEN becomes the WINDSWEPT QUESTANT.

The Exiles make peace, rebuild their tiny civilization, and use the command stations to guide the kids in the past.

Recap #3

When the WINDSWEPT QUESTANT arrives at the ruins, WV and AR offer her a crown made from mailboxes. She declines and places it on PM instead, honoring the delivery mission PM carried through the collapse of both kingdoms.

PM accepts the crown and becomes the Exiles’ new queen.

Recap #3

One last secret: WV has been carrying a RING OF ORBS FOURFOLD all along.

END OF ACT 4.
