HOMESTUCK RECAP #2

Act 3: Insane Corkscrew Haymakers

Intermission: Don’t Bleed on the Suits.

Act 3: Insane Corkscrew Haymakers

Act 3 finally introduces gardenGnostic (GG) in person as JADE HARLEY, the fourth member of the group. She lives on a remote tropical island with a sprawling house, a lagoon full of mysterious frog ruins, and an absurdly powerful dog named BECQUEREL.

Bec can teleport almost anywhere instantly and is extremely serious about keeping Jade away from those ruins.

Jade’s house is packed with her late GRANDPA’s exotic technology and trophies, including his own stuffed body. A DREAMBOT acts out her dreams in the waking world, records what she sees, and lets her stay connected to her friends while asleep.

She also seems to know things before they happen, keeping colorful reminders and following hunches whose source is not yet clear.

While Jade’s introduction unfolds, the other kids pick up where Act 2 left them. Dave fights his older brother, BRO, on the roof for the Sburb discs he needs to help Rose. John battles the CRUDE OGRES, while Rose follows the secret passage beneath Jaspers’s mausoleum toward the nearby laboratory.

Rose finds a SKAIANET laboratory whose computers track Sburb sessions and incoming meteors. It also houses machines for retrieving objects from the past and equipment for ECTOBIOLOGY, the creation of living clones.

Rose tries to retrieve a living Jaspers, but the machine prevents her from changing her own past. It produces paradox slime instead, which the cloning equipment turns into a malformed cat. Rose retrieves the original Jaspers’s corpse and escapes home with it and a separate mutant kitten she found wandering the lab.

With Rose reconnected, John defeats the ogres. In DAD’s distressingly normal room, he discovers that his father was an ordinary businessman, not the professional clown he always imagined. He realizes how much he had simply assumed.

John finally notices the graffiti on his own walls and makes new equipment while Rose builds toward the FIRST GATE. For now, he blames the imps for defacing his posters.

When Jade falls asleep, her DREAMSELF wakes on PROSPIT, the golden kingdom of light. Prospit orbits Skaia with a moon attached by a giant chain. Dream Jade lives on that moon and can fly freely through the golden city.

When Prospit’s moon swings close to Skaia, Dream Jade watches visions form in the clouds. This is where much of her strange foreknowledge comes from.

Dream John lives in the neighboring tower, but he has never awakened. His troubled dreams spill into waking life as the graffiti he unconsciously leaves on his walls.

In the future wasteland, PEREGRINE MENDICANT’s command station carries her to the same frog ruins where WV landed. Their meeting is complicated by a trigger-happy black-shelled stranger, the AIMLESS RENEGADE, who has declared the ruins a crime scene and would very much like everyone to stop contaminating it.

Jade’s dream visions have already set a complicated delivery route in motion. PM and WV help send a blue package through PM’s station machinery so it can arrive on Jade’s island years earlier. It is John’s birthday gift to Jade, delivered to her as a little girl, long before he ever sent it.

At roughly the same time, the kids’ chats reveal that they have been receiving messages from a group of hostile internet trolls. The trolls claim to be outside the kids’ universe, speak as though they know what will happen, and seem deeply committed to being annoying about it.

DAD, meanwhile, has been carried to the purple kingdom opposing Prospit. There he meets its ARCHAGENT, JACK NOIR, a black-shelled bureaucrat forced to wear increasingly humiliating harlequin attire. DAD earns Jack’s immediate respect by destroying the hat. Jack decides not to kill him.

Dave’s rooftop battle ends with BRO defeating him. Bro leaves the Sburb discs behind and rockets away on his board. Dave can finally become Rose’s server player and help her escape the approaching meteor, just as she helped John.

Dave installs Sburb and connects to Rose. Together they deploy her entry machinery and prototype her KERNELSPRITE twice, first with Jaspers and then with a tentacled princess doll.

Rose leaps after her falling cruxite wine bottle, and Jaspersprite pulls her to safety. She smashes the bottle to enter the Medium just before the meteor strikes.

Act 3 closes with several entries of a different sort. John reaches the FIRST GATE and jumps through. Jade enters the frog ruins for the first time and finds a flowering lotus time capsule, which opens to reveal a pair of mysteriously juice-stained Sburb discs.

END OF ACT 3.

Intermission: Don’t Bleed on the Suits.

The Intermission leaves the four kids behind for a violent mobster comedy. How it connects to their story is left hanging.

SPADES SLICK, DIAMONDS DROOG, CLUBS DEUCE, and HEARTS BOXCARS are the MIDNIGHT CREW, a gang of black-shelled mobsters. Their rivals, THE FELT, have hit one of their casinos, and the Crew has come to their mansion for revenge. Their agenda is straightforward: wreck the place, kill green guys, and crack a very large vault.

The Felt are fifteen mobsters employed by the mysterious LORD ENGLISH. Nearly all of them have some kind of time-related ability, including slowing time, following someone’s past or future trail, and jumping between timelines. The Crew responds mostly by shooting them.

One member of the Felt is different. SNOWMAN is tied to the universe itself, so killing her would destroy the universe too. This makes her difficult to murder even by Midnight Crew standards. She blinds Slick in one eye with her cigarette holder; killing her in revenge would take everyone else with her.

Eventually Slick loses patience and goes straight for the vault. Using CROWBAR’s crowbar on it triggers exactly the sort of weird time stuff Clover warned him about. Slick is thrown into another timeline where the mansion is already a smoking ruin and almost everyone is dead.

SNOWMAN is still there. She shoots Slick’s barcoded card, tears off his barcoded arm, and locks him inside the vault. Slick flips his character graphic to face the other way, conveniently moving the barcode onto his remaining arm. He scans it to open a hatch in the floor.

Below the vault, Slick finds a command station like WV’s and PM’s. Its monitors are tracking twelve unfamiliar players in the Medium. One screen shows a young troll, and Slick immediately starts typing commands at him.

Somehow, this was all relevant.

END INTERMISSION.
